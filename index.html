Personal Broadcast Recorder

Personal Broadcast Recorder is a non-commercial, single-user automation tool. It records selected publicly available livestreams and allows its operator to upload recordings to their own YouTube channel as private videos.

Why Google authorization is requested

The tool accesses the operator's YouTube account only to identify the intended upload channel, upload private video files, and verify video upload and processing status. The tool does not offer public user registration.

How it operates

Recording and processing run on the operator's privately managed server. Telegram is used for text notifications and links, not for storing uploaded recording files.
